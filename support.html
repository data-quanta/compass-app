If you are in immediate danger, or thinking about harming yourself, please don't use an app for this. Contact your local emergency number, or a crisis line — 988 (US & Canada, call or text), 116 123 (Samaritans, UK & Ireland), 13 11 14 (Lifeline, Australia) — or find the line for your country at findahelpline.com. The app's Help now tab has one-tap buttons for these.

What this app is

A free set of self-reflection tools: brief check-ins using questionnaires that are also used in research and primary care, guided work on habits and self-criticism, a 30-second daily mood log, and evidence-based coping techniques for hard moments. It is designed to help you notice your own patterns and try small, well-tested skills.

It is not therapy, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional care. If things are heavy or not shifting, talking to a professional is the stronger move — and going is a strength, not a failure.

Common questions

Do I need an account or an internet connection?
Neither. The app works fully offline and has nothing to sign up for.
Where is my data?
Only on your device. We can't see it. There's no cloud copy and no backup on our side — so if you want to keep something, use Save my plan or the JSON export in Pulse's Data tab before you delete the app.
Are the questionnaire scores a diagnosis?
No. They are screening and self-tracking tools. A score is a prompt to reflect, or to talk to a professional — never a diagnosis by itself.
What is the "mind-landscape"?
An interpretive picture that organises your own answers — a way of seeing which patterns look strongest to you. It is not a validated clinical instrument, and the app asks you to confirm or correct it, because you're the one who knows.
What about the mood variability signal in Pulse?
It describes how much your logged mood has been moving. It is explicitly exploratory: research has not shown that signals like this reliably predict anything for an individual, especially at one entry per day. Treat it as a curiosity, never a forecast or a warning.
How do reminders work?
They're optional local notifications on your device. Turn them on or off in Pulse → Data, or in iOS Settings.
Is it really free?
Yes — no purchases, no subscription, no ads, no data sold.
