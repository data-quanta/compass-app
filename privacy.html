Privacy Policy

Compass (Ψ Suite) · Last updated: 22 July 2026

This app collects nothing. There is no account, no cloud, no analytics, and no advertising. Everything you enter stays on your device. The app is free, and it is not funded by your data.

What we collect

Nothing. We operate no server that receives your information, because your use of the app does not involve a server. We cannot see your answers, your scores, your notes, or whether you use the app at all. There is no sign-up, no login, and no identifier of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your questionnaire answers and scores, daily check-ins, written notes, plans and commitments, and your settings are stored only in the app's local storage on your device. They are never transmitted to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it — so if you want to keep a copy, use the in-app Save my plan and the Pulse JSON export first.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily check-in reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device. It is handled entirely by your device's operating system. No reminder data is sent anywhere, and you can turn it off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Crisis resources

The app can show crisis phone lines relevant to your device's region. This uses only your device's regional setting, which the app reads locally; your location is not requested, collected, or transmitted. If you choose to call or text a line, your phone's own dialer or messaging app makes that connection — the app does not place the call, cannot record it, and is not told whether you made contact.

No third parties

The app contains no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party analytics SDKs. Nothing about you is sold or shared, because nothing about you leaves your device. If you tap a link to an external website (for example, an international crisis directory), that site has its own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Children

This app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Not medical care

Compass provides psychoeducation and self-reflection tools. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose, and is not a substitute for professional care or treatment. Questionnaire results are screening and self-tracking aids, never a diagnosis. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency services or a crisis line.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You hold the only copy, and you can export or erase it at any time from within the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
