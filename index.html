Compass

A free, private set of evidence-based self-reflection tools. No account, no cloud, no ads — everything you write stays on your phone.

  • Compass — a short check-in using questionnaires also used in research and primary care.
  • Roots — what keeps a habit or an inner critic going, and the matched approach.
  • Pulse — 30 seconds a day, turning into charts of how your mood actually moves.
  • Help now — one tap, for the hard moments.

Compass is psychoeducation and self-reflection — not therapy, not a diagnosis, and not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, please contact a crisis line or your local emergency number.
